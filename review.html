Weekly dictation review

Review up to 10 differing sentences. Listen, choose the correct text, and move on. Highlighted words differ. Unanswered items are skipped.

Choose Noise / not a dictation for background sounds or accidental recordings. An optional note is saved only with that choice; neither creates vocabulary or replacements.

Each item compares one inserted sentence with the re-check. Playback includes a short margin. Inserted text uses VoiceInk’s cleaned text when present; older reviews also show the original Whisper text. Stored text is the available insertion proxy.

Closes after five minutes without activity. Choices are saved only when you press Done.
